# Elite specialization reference research

The original damage-build edition is documented below. [Version 2 research](SPEC_TOOLS_RESEARCH.md) adds the two cozy lanes and practical tools; the original solo/group benchmark dataset and scoring formula remain available.

Snapshot: 17 October 2026 UTC. This document contains all specialization names, including later expansions. The website gates EoD and newest-expansion spec names independently of map spoiler controls.

## Coverage and evidence

The official API supplies the complete 36-specialization roster, nine each from HoT, PoF, EoD and VoE. Research reviewed 188 unique Snow Crows benchmark variants and 102 individual build pages from Snow Crows and MetaBattle. The selected website dataset has 92 distinct build records, reused when a power route is also the top pick. All selected source pages were retrieved successfully. Revision dates belong to the build content, not site navigation or related articles.

## Selection and limitations

Solo and group PvE are separate, equally accessible views. Solo choices use the MetaBattle editor tier first, then the displayed community score. Ties use a curated general-play choice (for example Deadeye's pistol reset build). Scores and editor tiers are separate signals and can disagree. New or Test entries are not silently promoted. Conduit and Luminary use explicitly editorial Snow Crows open-world picks because they have no entry in the reviewed MetaBattle index. The power Harbinger route also uses Snow Crows. Champion Hunter variants are more defensive choices, not claims of highest unbuffed DPS.

Group picks use the highest listed average benchmark of the reviewed damage variants for each specialization. This is a transparent benchmark criterion rather than a universal “best build” claim. Druid is explicitly marked as a condition-alacrity boon role because no pure-DPS alternative is listed. Highest benchmark does not establish best real-fight performance. Fractals are given group-role and agony-resistance caveats; this is not a fractal speedrun tier list. Dedicated healing/support recommendations are outside this damage-focused first edition.

No current recommended group power route was verified for Druid, Mirage, Scourge, Firebrand or Specter. Mirage has a sourced solo power variant. The other four also lack a recommended solo power route in the reviewed sources. These are documented evidence gaps, not claims that equipping power gear is impossible.

Weapons, exact stat slots, trait lines, available template codes, source tiers, build revision dates and group benchmark average/max are factual source fields. Snow Crows utility-skill names were resolved through the official skill API. Build codes import traits/skills, never equipment. Source links retain the complete rune/sigil/relic/food setup and full rotation; the local reference provides a concise gameplay orientation rather than reproducing entire build guides. Cross-expansion weapon access must be checked against ownership and training before purchases.

## Editorial scores

All subjective scores are desk-research estimates, not personal playtest measurements. 1–5 scales: complexity (simultaneous systems/input demands), skill floor (difficulty achieving useful output), ceiling (optimization depth), flow (continuity), fun (preference for active expressive gameplay), class fantasy (how clearly the theme is expressed through mechanics), and combat power.

High complexity/floor are informational, not directly penalized in the overall score. High ceiling is positive. Overall = (fantasy + ceiling + fun + combat power) / 4, displayed to two decimals. Equal scores are ties; alphabetical tie-breaking affects only the single leader highlight. Reaper is an explicit editorial class-fantasy choice, with reasoning, while other strong themes can also score 5/5. There is no objectively best fantasy.

Group combat power equals a published average DPS band: 5 >=44,000; 4 >=40,000; 3 >=36,000; 2 below 36,000. Utility/boon contribution is not added, so boon roles should be compared within their role. Benchmarks assume their source setup and do not predict personal or encounter output. Maximum and average remain separate. Solo combat power is a qualitative editorial score considering useful offence, self buffs and continued attacking; there are no invented solo DPS measurements. Scores across contexts have different evidence bases and are not a claim of equivalent raw output.

Source difficulty, stated learning/mastery demands and mechanical friction inform the build-specific estimates. Fun/fantasy are primarily spec-level preferences; exact variants can have different complexity and floor. Future editions should revisit scores with recorded playtesting and add encounter-specific recommendations.
