# Independent whole-route review · 18 October 2026

Reviewed the 25 default challenges and six reserve records against embedded map geometry and fresh indexed GW2 Wiki pages. This is a document/source audit, not an in-game traversal or proof of fastest routing.

## Confirmed correction

The Verdant Brink entry gate and shared atlas map entry incorrectly locate the portal **in Tangled Labyrinth**. The normal entry is the southwestern Silverwastes surface door behind Amber Sandfall. Tangled Labyrinth is a separate underground area available during the post-Vinewrath timeout; sending first-time players there creates a needless event-gate detour. Retain the introductory-story access requirement, but explicitly say to use the surface door and not enter the labyrinth or wait for Vinewrath.

Sources:

- https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Tangled_Labyrinth

## Checks with no further contradiction found

- All 31 selected/reserve challenge coordinates are in the expected compass quadrant from their assigned waypoint. Coordinates establish direction only, not walkable terrain or vertical access.
- Desert Highlands challenge list records ordinary Springer access for Glint's Lair, Eternal Fire, Glowing Campfire and Branded Crystal. It does not list High Vault for them. Glint's individual page describes scaling the walls beside the corresponding point of interest. Eternal Fire has separate route illustrations from the Sunderlands with Raptor/Springer and from the tomb with Springer/gliding. There is no source basis here to assert that High Vault is mandatory.
- Cliffside Ruins explicitly supports ramps followed by a drop/glide with Glider Basics.
- Abyssal Depths individual notes describe gliding with Updraft Use helpful, while the map challenge list records Glider Basics. The reported player kit includes Updraft Use; no additional mastery was established as mandatory by these sources.
- Coztic Itzel Belongings is northeast of Treacherous Path near the Auric Basin portal. Its repeat event awards daily resources, not another set of hero points.
- Young Mushrooms is a genuine champion event, reached by dropping south of Wanderer's; defeat within its ten-minute event limit gives ten hero points on first completion.
- Toxin-Cured Hog's northern drop avoids the vine-blocked southern entrance. Its own healing skill can complete it; use after the fifth damage pulse, then on recharge. Failure resets health. The guide's treatment is supported.
- Crystal Desert challenge list identifies all selected challenges as ten-point challenges. Raptor/Springer requirements in that list fit the reported travel kit; no selected challenge was found to require Jackal, flying mounts or later story chapters.
