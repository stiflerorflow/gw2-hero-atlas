Reviewed 18 October 2026 against da90783. Scope: all 16 main PoF stops, incoming links and outbound transitions. This is source and map review, not an in-game traversal. ## Finding requiring a conservative correction

**Forgotten Shrine → Branded Relic:** the current continuous text assumes a northeast departure from the shrine after its Springer/glider descent. Neither the indexed individual Wiki articles nor the fetched GuildJen text establishes that exit. Return to the already-confirmed Augury’s Shadow waypoint, then take the separate northeast Raptor approach to Branded Relic. This avoids an unsupported reverse-climb assumption. Root has accepted this correction. Do not describe the old link as freshly verified.

The end-of-desert transfer also needs a concrete way to reach core Tyria when no suitable waypoint is known; “travel to the Silverwastes” plus “walk from Brisban” does not explain leaving Elona. Verify a free core-return method before adding one, or explicitly distinguish unknown expansion waypoints from already-known core waypoints.

## Makali → Lommuld fallback clarification

A further fresh check corrects the landmark wording: do not prescribe travelling **through Highjump Gap** to reach Lommuld. The [Bahir article](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Bahir) places him east of the gap trying to reach the ranch westward; [Stampede Uplands](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Stampede_Uplands) describes that Canyon Jumping crossing as access to the ranch. [Salt Flats](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Salt_Flats) connects directly north to Auburn Hills.

Fresh [official map1211 metadata](https://api.guildwars2.com/v2/continents/1/floors/49/regions/12/maps/1211) gives Gap [59929.5,40441.6], Ranch [59709.4,40428] to its west, and Lommuld waypoint [60598.2,40452.9] to its east. Makali waypoint [60676.5,42149.4] is south. Embedded official terrain also inspected. Safer bounded instruction: “From Makali, follow the northbound route through Salt Flats into Auburn Hills. Keep to the east side of Highjump Gap rather than crossing west toward Highjump Ranch; continue toward Lommuld Kraal and discover its waypoint.” This supplies supported regional landmarks without inventing exact road turns or a mandatory ranch detour.
